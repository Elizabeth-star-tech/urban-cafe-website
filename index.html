Our story

A neighbourhood table with a warm welcome.

Urban Cafe began with a simple idea: serve the kind of food you'd want to share with family, in a space that feels like an easy extension of home.

Our kitchen cooks to order — from hearty breakfasts and slow-grilled meats to fragrant rice dishes and fresh chapati — using ingredients picked up locally each morning. The coffee is ground fresh, the portions are generous, and the team knows most guests by name.

Whether you're stopping in after shopping next door at Naivas, breaking a long drive, or gathering the family for a weekend lunch, there's a seat waiting for you.
